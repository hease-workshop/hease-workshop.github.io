## About HEASE

An agent harness gives a language model the tools, context, memory, and feedback it needs to carry out multi-step software engineering tasks. HEASE brings researchers and practitioners together to study how harness design affects agent performance, efficiency, and reliability, and how to evaluate those effects rigorously and reproducibly.
