# Call for Papers

HEASE 2027 invites research contributions, empirical studies, and experience reports on the design, development, evaluation, and assessment of agent harnesses for software engineering.

## Topics of interest

- **Harness architectures and design** · Architectures, frameworks, techniques, and design patterns.
- **Harness components** · Tool integration, context management, memory, skills, planning, and feedback.
- **Empirical evaluation** · Controlled experiments, comparative studies, and ablations that isolate harness effects.
- **Benchmarks and methodology** · Metrics and evaluation frameworks for effectiveness, efficiency, and reliability.
- **Reproducibility and generalizability** · Experimental reporting, replications, and evaluation across models and tasks.
- **Performance and efficiency** · Task success, token consumption, inference costs, and execution time.
- **Reliability, robustness, and security** · Behavior under failures, changing environments, and security constraints.
- **Industrial applications** · Deployment challenges, practical experience, and lessons from real-world use.
- **Open challenges** · Emerging research directions in agent harness engineering.

## Submission guidelines

Use the [ACM proceedings template](https://www.acm.org/publications/proceedings-template) with the `sigconf` format.

- **Full papers (up to 10 pages)** report novel research, empirical studies, replications, or benchmark evaluations.
- **Short papers (up to 5 pages)** present early results, new ideas, or industrial experience reports.

Submissions will go through EasyChair. The submission link will be announced here. At least three program committee members will review each paper in a single-blind process.

Authors of accepted papers will submit a camera-ready version. At least one author of each accepted paper must register for EASE 2027 and present the work at the workshop.

See the [important dates]({{ '/dates' | relative_url }}) for submission, notification, and camera-ready deadlines.
